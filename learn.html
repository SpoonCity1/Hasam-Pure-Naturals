Pineapple, a Ghanaian favourite

Vitamin C and a tropical taste

Pineapple is a good source of vitamin C and also contains bromelain, a natural enzyme found in the fruit. Ghana is well known for its sweet pineapples, which is why they make such a good base for juice.

Serve it cold, and shake the bottle gently before you pour.

Oranges and vitamin C

A classic for a reason

Oranges are rich in vitamin C, which the body uses every day to look after skin and support normal immune function. Fresh orange juice also gives you a little natural energy from the fruit sugars.

Best served chilled. Keep an opened bottle in the fridge and finish it within the time on the label.

Bananas for steady energy

Potassium and natural sweetness

Bananas provide potassium, fibre and natural sugars, so they are a popular choice before school, work or exercise. Blended into a drink, they make it thick and filling.

Getting the best from herbal and aromatic teas

Ginger, cloves, cinnamon and lemongrass

Spices such as ginger, cloves and cinnamon, and herbs such as lemongrass, have been enjoyed in warm drinks for generations for their aroma and comfort.

To brew: add one heaped teaspoon or one tea bag to a cup, pour over hot water, cover, and steep for 3 to 5 minutes. Add honey or lemon if you like.

Enjoying dried fruits

Small handful, big flavour

Drying removes the water and concentrates the natural sweetness, so a small handful goes a long way. Dried fruits are light and easy to carry for school, work or travel.

Store them in an airtight container in a cool, dry place, and they will keep their flavour longer.

Build a better breakfast parfait

Yoghurt, fruit and crunch

Yoghurt gives you protein and calcium, fruit adds vitamins and fibre, and a little granola brings the crunch. Layer them in a cup for a breakfast or snack that is easy to eat on the go.

Keep parfaits chilled and eat them the same day for the best texture.

Boba tea, done right

Best fresh and cold

Boba pearls are softest and chewiest in the first few hours after they are made, so enjoy your boba tea on the day you buy it. Use a wide straw, and treat it as a fun, occasional drink.
